Hi, I’m Jason. I’m the cofounder and CTO of Atomscale+ From atom to scale. AI for materials synthesis: connecting process data, understanding material state, and helping engineers control fabrication in real time. Explore Atomscale ↗ , where we’re building the intelligence layer for atomic-scale engineering.

I work at the intersection of materials science, AI/ML, and scientific software. I’m interested in closing the loop between what we can predict about a material and what we can reliably make—from the first experiment to production.

Previously, I co-led technical development of the Materials Project+ Making materials data useful. An open database and software ecosystem for discovering and understanding materials, making computed properties and scientific tools available to researchers worldwide. Visit the Materials Project ↗ at Berkeley Lab, helping grow the world’s most-cited materials database and software stack to over half a million users.

I have a PhD in materials science and engineering and a bachelor’s in engineering physics. My doctoral and postdoctoral research spanned materials physics, AI/ML, and data-driven approaches to materials discovery+ Physics, learning, and data. My work connects materials physics with AI/ML and large-scale datasets to understand how materials behave, predict their properties, and guide discovery. Google scholar ↗ .
